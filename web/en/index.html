FAQ

What is KampusLink?

A study app for Algerian university students: short lessons, smart flashcards, exam-trap quizzes, solved exercises and mock exams.

Who is it for?

Right now, L1 Sciences et Technologies students (common core, all ST specialities) and L2 Génie minier students. Bac is coming soon.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, through the web version: open kampus-link.com in your browser. On Android, get the app on Google Play.

Can I study offline?

Yes. Once content is loaded, the app works without internet.

Is it linked to my university?

No. KampusLink is an independent study tool, not affiliated with any university.

How do the flashcards work?

With the Leitner system (spaced repetition): a card you miss comes back soon, a card you know comes back later. You review at the right time and waste none.
